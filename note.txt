What Does It Mean to "Update Your CSS"?
Whenever you:
Change your HTML and use new Tailwind classes
Update your src/input.css with new custom styles
Change your Tailwind config (e.g., add colors, fonts, etc.)
You need to rebuild your CSS so that Tailwind can:
Scan your files for all the classes you use
Remove unused styles (purge)
Output a new, optimized assets/css/tailwind.css
How to do it:
Just run:

npx tailwindcss -i ./src/input.css -o ./assets/css/tailwind.css --minify

Or, if you want it to watch for changes as you develop:
npx tailwindcss -i ./src/input.css -o ./assets/css/tailwind.css --watch

In short:
Whenever you make changes to your HTML or Tailwind config, "update your CSS" means to re-run the Tailwind build command so your site always uses the latest, optimized styles.
